EMPLOYEE SALARY SHEET - PROPOSED IMPORT TEMPLATE

These files are synthetic assessment examples, not existing employee records.
The app does not yet have a CSV/Excel upload endpoint. Do not paste CSV into
HR policy text: that saves a policy document, not employee salary records.

Each row represents one employee and one payroll month.
- employeeId: unique employee identifier.
- year: payroll year, for example 2026.
- month: 1 = January through 12 = December; 11 means November.
- annualCtcMinor: annual CTC in integer currency minor units.
- currency: supported ISO currency code, for example INR.
- unpaidLeaveDays: whole unpaid days within the selected calendar month.
- otherDeductionsMinor: deductions for this month in integer minor units.

For INR, 100 minor units = 1 rupee.
DEMO-001 has annual CTC of INR 12,00,000, represented as 120000000 paise.
DEMO-002 has annual CTC of INR 9,60,000, represented as 96000000 paise.
DEMO-003 has annual CTC of INR 6,00,000, represented as 60000000 paise.
Amounts are annual CTC, not monthly salary. Monthly pay is calculated by the app.
No reimbursement claims or login passwords are included in this template.

The proposed import flow is: choose sheet, validate, review rows/errors,
confirm, save employees and monthly payslips, refresh the dashboard.
Employee salary records and employee login accounts are separate concerns.
