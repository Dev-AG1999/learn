HR SALARY REGISTER - GOOGLE SHEETS WORKFLOW

Contains 20 fictional employees for 11/2026. Replace sample rows with
your employee IDs and payroll inputs before using real data.

1. Open Google Sheets and import hr-maintained-salary-register.xlsx.
2. Maintain one row per employee, using one payroll month for the whole file.
3. Keep the seven header names. Do not add names or extra columns to the upload tab.
4. Download that tab as CSV, or download the single-sheet workbook as Excel .xlsx.
5. In Paydesk, sign in as HR, choose Upload salary sheet, select the download,
   validate, review and confirm. The dashboard then shows saved monthly payroll.

Money columns ending in Minor use paise for INR.
Annual CTC Rs 12,00,000 = annualCtcMinor 120000000.
A monthly deduction of Rs 1,500 = otherDeductionsMinor 150000.
Do not put monthly salary into the annual CTC column.

Month: 1 January, 2 February, 3 March, 4 April, 5 May, 6 June,
7 July, 8 August, 9 September, 10 October, 11 November, 12 December.

Each ID must be unique within a file. Re-uploading existing IDs updates only
the chosen payroll month after review; other saved months and claims are kept.
For another month, change year/month for every row, or maintain a separate
monthly Google Sheet. If you keep multiple tabs, export only the selected
month as CSV: the app accepts only single-worksheet Excel files.

Use plain values, not formulas, in the upload file. Keep employee IDs as text
to preserve leading zeros. No passwords, bank details or claim approvals belong
in this template. Employee login accounts are created separately.

This workflow uploads an exported file; it does not automatically sync Google
Sheets. The application does not currently map arbitrary legacy sheet columns.
