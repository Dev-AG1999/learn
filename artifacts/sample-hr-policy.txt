Reimbursement and monthly payroll
Document ID: sample-reimbursement-policy
Assessment sample - not an approved company policy

Purpose
This sample document describes the reimbursement and payroll workflow in the HR salary management assessment. It contains no real employee data and is not an approved company policy.

Submitting a claim
Employees submit a reimbursement claim for their own payroll record. Each claim includes an amount in whole currency minor units and a receipt reference. New claims remain pending until reviewed by HR.

HR approval
Only HR can approve a reimbursement claim. A pending claim does not change net pay. Once approved, the reimbursement is included through the payroll calculation and saved in the monthly payslip.

Monthly payroll
Monthly gross is annual CTC divided by twelve. Unpaid leave deductions and other deductions entered by HR reduce pay. Paid leave does not reduce pay. Approved reimbursements increase net pay.

Saved payslips and access
Employees can view and download only their own payslips. HR manages payroll inputs in the HR workspace. Saving another payroll month does not rewrite an earlier monthly snapshot.

Questions for the policy lookup
Try these exact phrases in the HR policy search: pending claim, receipt reference, paid leave, or monthly snapshot. The current lookup matches text in saved documents and returns the document ID as its citation.
